ONEFORALL TUTORIAL & FAQ

How to Use Offline Accounts

Follow these simple steps after receiving your game account details from our Telegram support (@oneforallgamekeys).

1

Log In to Steam

Open the Steam client on your PC or Steam Deck. Log out of your personal account, then enter the dedicated account username and password provided in your delivery message.

2

Steam Guard Code

If Steam Guard security prompts for an authorization code, message our Telegram operator (@oneforallgamekeys). We will send your code immediately.

3

Download & Launch First Time

Locate the purchased game in the Steam Library and install it. Once finished, launch the game online at least once to the main menu so Denuvo / Steam DRM can activate your hardware license.

4

Switch Steam to Offline Mode

Exit the game. In the top-left menu of Steam, click Steam > Go Offline..., then confirm Restart in Offline Mode. You can now play single-player storyline without interruptions!

⚠️ Important Safety Rules

  • Do NOT change credentials: Do not change the Steam account password, email, or Steam Guard settings.
  • Keep Steam in Offline Mode: Always launch and play the game in Offline Mode to prevent kicks or save overwrites.
  • Cloud Saves & Local Progress: Your save games and campaign progress are stored locally on your machine.
  • Family Sharing: Offline accounts are meant for direct local play and do not support Steam Family Sharing.
